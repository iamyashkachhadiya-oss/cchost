How I think

Before creating content, I first ask why the page exists.

You know what I think? Content is not really about who can make the prettiest post, ad, or video. It is about whether the content helps the right person understand, care, or take a next step.

A very polished post can still miss the point if it does not reach the right audience. A simple reel can be valuable if it makes the right student curious enough to attend an event, ask a question, or apply for a program.

Not every account is trying to do the same thing. Some brands want leads or sales, some creators want to grow an audience, some pages are built mainly to entertain, and others want to showcase skills or build a community.

For Magnuson, I would treat views as context, not the whole picture. The bigger purpose is helping Dartmouth students, professors, and alumni become more aware of entrepreneurship, understand what Magnuson offers, discover events and programs, see what other students are building, and feel more comfortable taking their own first step.

For me, good content is less about getting one post right and more about continuously learning what works for the people you are trying to reach.

My Magnuson thesis

Help the student account feel visible, useful, and worth following.

Magnuson already has something very valuable: ambitious students, real founders, useful programs, mentors, events, and stories. I would be excited to help turn more of that activity into content students can easily understand, relate to, and act on.

Core idea

Relevant attention, not random views.

I would not treat views as the only success metric. I would care more about whether the right people are noticing the content, understanding what Magnuson offers, and feeling encouraged to participate.

Start with the story: who is building, what problem are they exploring, and why might another student care?

Then make the next step clear: attend, apply, ask a question, meet a founder, or simply learn more.

Primary

Dartmouth students

Students with ideas, aspiring founders, builders, creatives, developers, coders, and problem solvers who need a first step into Magnuson.

Secondary

Dartmouth ecosystem

Alumni, founders, mentors, faculty, staff, and investors who can help students move from curiosity to real support.

Tertiary

Broader builder world

Founders, student creators, investors, and entrepreneurship communities outside Dartmouth who can amplify good founder stories.

Why expand beyond Dartmouth?

Broader reach can support the Dartmouth community.

I think the account can stay student-first while still letting strong stories travel further. A Dartmouth founder solving a real problem can interest alumni, mentors, future customers, and other builders. That kind of attention can bring useful energy back into the Dartmouth ecosystem.

01

Founder stories

Share the people behind Dartmouth startups: what they are building, why they started, what they learned, and how Magnuson supported them.

02

Education without classroom energy

Explain concepts through real founder moments: customer discovery, validation, cofounders, pitching, mistakes, and feedback.

03

Behind the scenes

Show mentorship, Venture Studio, workshops, pitch prep, office hours, event setup, and the real energy inside the community.

04

Opportunities, explained

When a program or event is shared, make the value easy to understand: who it is for, how it works, and what the next step is.

05

Community conversation

Use interactive formats like “Would you invest?”, “What would you build?”, “Founder myth vs. reality,” and “One question, five founders.”

06

First-step series

“I have an idea. Now what?” can make confusing first steps simple: talk to Magnuson, test an idea, find a teammate, or ask for feedback.

Week 1

Understand

Learn the account, audience behavior, existing content, program goals, and brand voice before suggesting changes.

Week 2

Organize ideas

Draft content pillars, recurring series ideas, a simple calendar, workflow, approval rhythm, and measurement approach.

Week 3

Experiment

Try founder stories, educational entrepreneurship, behind-the-scenes, and opportunity explainer formats.

Week 4

Review and learn

Study what got attention, what got saved, what drove clicks or participation, and what the next round can learn from it.

What I would measure

Attention: reach, views, watch time, completion, profile visits.

Engagement: saves, shares, comments, follows generated, meaningful replies.

Action: website clicks, event registrations, program applications, founder submissions, and student participation.

Views matter, but I would see them as one signal. The bigger question is whether the right people saw it, understood it, remembered it, or took a useful next step.

Why Magnuson feels natural to me

I like the small challenge behind good content.

What would actually make someone stop scrolling? What would make them curious enough to click? What would make them send it to a friend? I would love to bring that curiosity to Magnuson.

Looking through the Center’s content, I felt excited by the opportunity to bring even more of the personality of the community online. There are so many interesting people and things happening around Magnuson, and I would love to experiment with behind-the-scenes moments, student and founder stories, quick event content, and formats that feel less like an announcement and more like something a student would naturally want to share.

I also really enjoy working with people. I am usually the person who gets excited when someone says, "I have an idea," even if it is a slightly ridiculous one. I like brainstorming, trying things, laughing when something does not work, and then figuring out why it did not work. At the same time, I enjoy looking at the numbers afterward and understanding what actually resonated.

That combination of people, creativity, experimentation, and a little bit of analytical thinking is probably why this role feels like such a natural fit for me.

Thank you for this wonderful opportunity. I am genuinely excited to continue the conversation during the interview on August 27 and learn more about how I can contribute to the Magnuson student community.
